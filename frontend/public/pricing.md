# Pricing — Orbita

Last updated: 2026-08-25
Currency: INR (default for India) and USD. Billed per company, not per discovered agent.
Free scan: no credit card. Paid plans billed in advance monthly. GST extra where applicable.
Human page: https://www.orbita.io/pricing
Contact: hello@orbita.io

## Free discovery scan
- Price: $0 / ₹0
- Limits: up to 3 read-only sources
- Features: full agent inventory, owner mapping, risk scores; you keep the report
- CTA: https://www.orbita.io/signup

## Starter
- Price: ₹40,000/month | $1,500/month
- Limits: up to 100 agents, 5 connectors
- Features: weekly discovery scans, email alerts, DPDP starter report
- CTA: https://www.orbita.io/signup

## Growth
- Price: ₹80,000/month | $3,000/month
- Limits: unlimited agents, all connectors
- Features: continuous monitoring, kill switch + approvals, all compliance packs (DPDP, SOC 2, ISO 27001, EU AI Act), peer benchmarks, auditor role
- CTA: https://www.orbita.io/signup
- Note: most popular paid plan

## Enterprise
- Price: custom — contact hello@orbita.io
- Limits: unlimited
- Features: self-hosted / data residency, Keycloak SSO, custom connectors, dedicated CSM, auditor workspace
- CTA: https://www.orbita.io/contact

## Notes
- No per-agent tax: finding more shadow AI does not increase the bill.
- Data residency: India (AWS Mumbai) by default; EU and US regions available; Enterprise can self-host.
- Connectors are read-only. No SDK install.
